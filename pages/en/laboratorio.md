The lab is the workbench: this is where things get **built**, **broken** and **investigated**. Every entry is a reproducible, documented, version-controlled step — no loose screenshots without context.

This first series is the **manual** route: standing up an Active Directory lab by hand, from bare metal to GOAD, with two years of historical noise and a forensic-imaging pipeline. The groundwork on which, later, each case is walked in three acts: <span style="color:var(--red)">the crime</span>, <span style="color:var(--green)">the defense</span> and <span style="color:var(--blue)">the investigation</span>.
