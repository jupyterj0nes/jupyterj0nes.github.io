The lab is the blog's workbench: an Active Directory network where the attack each article talks about is replayed and the evidence it leaves is collected. Everything you see in the screenshots on this site comes from there.

There are two ways to have one.

## Automatic deployment <span class="soon-tag">coming soon</span>

A whole network brought up and collected with a couple of commands, with each article's scenario ready to go. It is on its way; when it lands, every tool and artifact article will have its "Try it yourself" section filled in.

## Manual build, on bare metal

The long way, and the one that teaches the most: eleven instalments to build an Active Directory lab by hand, from a bare server to GOAD, with two years of historical noise and its forensic imaging process.

[See the whole series →](/en/laboratorio/manual/)
